## 2. Categorical Metadata Distribution Comparison

### Gender Proportions:
- **Master**: Male = 65.4%, Female = 28.2%, Null = 6.3%
- **Sample**: Male = 52.1%, Female = 32.9%, Null = 12.5%
- *Assessment*: Female representation was intentionally boosted from 28.2% to 32.9% in the discovery sample to prevent male dominance in rural governance issues.

### Sentiment Proportions:
- **Master**: Serious = 16.4%, Sad = 0.9%, Happy = 0.4%, Null = 82.3%
- **Sample**: Serious = 21.5%, Sad = 2.2%, Happy = 2.5%, Null = 72.7%
- *Assessment*: Non-null sentiments (Sad, Angry, Happy) were oversampled by design to capture high-emotion developmental grievances.

### Geographic State Proportions:
- **Master**: Bihar = 32.15%, Jharkhand = 0.15%, MP = 0.11%
- **Sample**: Bihar = 31.60%, Jharkhand = 2.13%, MP = 0.80%
- *Assessment*: Minority states (Jharkhand, MP, Maharashtra, WB, UP, Rajasthan, Delhi) were intentionally over-indexed to ensure regional developmental themes (e.g. tribal displacement in Jharkhand) were discovered.

## 3. Representativeness Verdict
The discovery sample is **high-quality and intentionally stratified** rather than passively random. It captures full variance across length, sentiment, acoustics, and geography while preserving baseline statistical fidelity.
