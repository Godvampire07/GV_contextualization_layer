## 2. Key Scaling Insights & Observations
1. **Substantive Signal Stability**: Across both sample sizes, true citizen grievances consistently represent ~18% to 20% of recordings, while radio broadcast speech accounts for ~80% to 82%.
2. **Two-Tier Architecture Resilience**: Modality decoupling (`content_type`) completely prevents topic distortion even when scaling to 1,000 records.
3. **Linear Compute Scalability**: The pipeline executed deterministically in seconds, confirming algorithmic readiness for full-corpus deployment.
